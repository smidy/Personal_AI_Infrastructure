# QuestForge Archetype Catalog

Reusable game-design templates. Each fuses a **benefit into the core loop** (not points bolted onto a
worksheet) and is **themeable to any interest**. The GENERATE step picks 1-2 archetypes per confirmed
help-target, then skins them. Extend this catalog freely — keep the "benefit IS the loop" rule.

| # | Archetype | Core loop | Benefit | Mechanism (why it works) | Themeability hook |
|---|-----------|-----------|---------|--------------------------|-------------------|
| 1 | **The Cartographer** | Navigate and redraw a self-erasing map, holding/updating spatial sequences under decay | Cognitive | Forces active maintenance + updating — the real working-memory construct; decay tuned to flow | Map = a world from a favorite BOOK/MOVIE |
| 2 | **Echo Keeper** | Guard a fading library; re-summon each "memory" at widening intervals or the world dims | Cognitive | Spaced retrieval IS the win condition — forgetting causes visible decay, so retrieval is intrinsic | "Memories" = facts from a GOAL domain (a language, finance) |
| 3 | **The Tending** | A living world (garden/colony/ship) thrives only when you log a real keystone habit; miss a day and it wilts — never game-overs | Behavioral | Cue-routine-reward + gentle loss-aversion without shame | The world = the user's MISSION universe |
| 4 | **The Pact** *(analog/paper-friendly)* | Draw quest cards that convert a stalled real GOAL into an "if-X-then-Y" mission with a witness and a stake | Behavioral | Implementation intentions (Gollwitzer) — among the strongest behavior-change tools | Card flavor over real PROJECTS; playable as a physical deck |
| 5 | **Threshold** | A self-paced ladder of micro-dares in a safe simulated space; each rung slightly harder; retreat always allowed | Emotional | Graded, player-paced exposure — core of evidence-based anxiety care; pacing prevents flooding | A BOOK/MOVIE hero facing fears |
| 6 | **Tide** | A short, bounded, high-absorption flow puzzle to play when mood dips | Emotional | Visuospatial absorption competes with intrusive imagery (Tetris effect, Holmes/James); induces flow | Palette/soundscape from calming MOVIES; no narrative pressure |
| 7 | **The Other Voice** | Coach an NPC who carries YOUR limiting belief; discover counter-evidence through play; the NPC's growth mirrors back | Mindset | Self-distancing — a proven reframing technique; you teach the reframe you need | NPC = a character archetype from a favorite NARRATIVE |
| 8 | **Epic Self** | A long-arc hero campaign where in-game "epic wins" unlock ONLY by logging real acts toward your MISSION, optionally witnessed by an ally guild | Mindset + Social | McGonigal "epic wins" + identity-based motivation + social accountability | You are the protagonist of your MISSION in your favorite genre |

## Selection guidance

- Match the **benefit category of the confirmed help-target** (see `BenefitMap.md`) to an archetype's benefit
  column. Cognitive targets → 1-2; behavioral → 3-4; emotional → 5-6; mindset/social → 7-8.
- **Prefer behavioral/emotional/mindset/social archetypes** over the cognitive pair unless the user explicitly
  wants skill drilling — those outcomes transfer to real life; cognitive "training" gains largely don't
  (`References/Evidence.md`).
- **Composition is allowed:** e.g. *Epic Self* (motivation) wrapping *The Tending* (habit) for a stalled GOAL.
- **At least one analog option:** *The Pact* needs no code — reach for it when "playable today" matters more
  than polish, or as the prototype fallback.

## Anti-patterns (reject these)

- "X but with points/badges/leaderboards" — extrinsic wrapper, no fused benefit.
- A quiz with a theme reskin and nothing else.
- Any loop where the "therapeutic" part is a menu/journal bolted beside the game rather than the gameplay.
